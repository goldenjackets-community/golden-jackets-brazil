## 1. Your Role

As Co-Founder, you help Ricardo (Founder) with:

- Reviewing and approving new member applications
- Creating Lounge access for verified members
- Managing the member list (promote challengers, remove inactive)
- Ensuring member data quality (verify Credly badges)

You do NOT manage:
- Site code or infrastructure
- International chapters
- Partnerships or sponsorships

---

## 2. Accessing the Admin Panel

1. Go to: https://goldenjacketsbrazil.com/members.html
2. Log in with your email
3. Click the **⚙️ Admin** button (top right)

---

## 3. Admin Tools Available

### 📋 List Users

Shows all Lounge members in Brazil with their status (Confirmed or Pending).

### ➕ Create User

Creates Lounge access for a new verified member:
1. Click **Create User**
2. Enter their name and email
3. Select type: Golden Jacket (12/12), Challenger (11/12), or Challenger (10/12)
4. Click **Create & Send Invite**
5. They receive an email with temporary password

### 🔄 Resend Pending

Resends invitation emails to members who haven't logged in yet.

### 🗑️ Delete User

Removes a member's Lounge access permanently.

### ⬆️ Promote Challenger

When a Challenger completes their 12th certification:
1. Click **Promote Challenger**
2. Enter their email
3. Reminder: you still need to ask Ricardo to update their card on the site

### 📊 Export CSV

Exports the member list as CSV for reporting.

### 📈 Show Metrics

Shows site visitor stats.

### 📋 Backlog

Shows the project backlog.

## 4. Member Validation Process

Before creating a user, **always verify**:

1. **Check Credly:** Confirm they have all 12 active AWS certifications
   - Go to their Credly profile (usually linked on LinkedIn)
   - Verify all 12 badges are active (not expired)

2. **Check LinkedIn:** Confirm identity matches

3. **If Challenger:** Verify they have 10 or 11 certifications

4. **Only after verification:** Create their Lounge user

**Never create a user without verifying their certifications.**

---

## 5. Rules & Guidelines

- Always verify before creating users
- Don't delete users without consulting Ricardo first
- Don't share admin access credentials
- If unsure about a decision, ask Ricardo
- Be welcoming — every new member chose to be part of this community
